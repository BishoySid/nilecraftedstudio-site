Our Story

More than gifts.
Memories that last.

Nile Crafted Studio creates personalized apparel, gifts, and keepsakes for the moments that matter most: birthdays, new babies, weddings, milestones, and everyday reasons to celebrate.

Every piece is designed around your story and finished with care, so it feels as personal as the moment behind it.

Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Randa

A wife, a mom of three beautiful children, a certified wedding and event planner, and the holder of a Master's degree in Social Work and International Education.

Creativity, design, and bringing joy to others have always been part of my passion. Nile Crafted Studio was created to turn meaningful moments into personalized creations that people can cherish forever.

From custom apparel and elegant gifts to event details and personalized keepsakes, every piece is made with love, creativity, and attention to detail. Thank you for supporting my small business and being part of this journey.
