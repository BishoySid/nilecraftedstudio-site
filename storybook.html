Prologue

Once upon a time

In a little studio on Staten Island, a wife, a mother of three and a certified wedding and event planner turned her love of creativity into keepsakes.

There, names and dates and small details become pieces you can hold: apparel, gifts and keepsakes for the moments that matter most.
