Privacy note

What we keep, and why

Last updated November 7, 2026

When you sign in to the storybook

To open Randa's storybook you sign in with Apple or Google. We then keep:

  • your name and email address, as Apple or Google shares them (if you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see Apple's private relay address)
  • which of the two you used
  • when you first visited, when you last visited, and how many times

We never see or store your Apple or Google password.

Why

So Randa knows who has looked through her work and can follow up if you'd like a piece of your own. We won't add you to a mailing list without asking you first, and we don't sell or share this information.

Where it's kept

Sign-in and this short visitor list are handled by Google Firebase. Your browser also remembers that you're signed in, so the book opens straight away next time. You can sign out from the storybook page at any time.

Orders and messages

When you send a custom order request, it goes from your own email or messages app to ours, and stays in those conversations.

Removing your information

Email nilecraftedstudio@gmail.com or text (862) 414-0499 and we'll delete it.
